## English

Maheshwari Calendar is an offline calendar app.

- **We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts or logins.
- The app sends no data over the internet. It contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- Your preferences (e.g. reminder time) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you
  uninstall the app.
- The **notification permission** is used solely to show festival reminders, which are scheduled
  locally on your device.
- Advertisements shown in the app are bundled with the app. Tapping one may open your phone dialer,
  WhatsApp, email or browser; those services' own privacy policies apply.
- The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
