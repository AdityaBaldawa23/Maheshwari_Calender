पर्व सूची) Google Firebase से डाउनलोड करता है। इसके लिए कोई पहचान नहीं भेजी जाती; सामान्य इंटरनेट अनुरोध की तरह Google के सर्वर
आपका IP पता देख सकते हैं।

**माहेश्वरी परिवार निर्देशिका:** इसमें समाज के सदस्यों की जानकारी (नाम, माता-पिता के नाम, शहर, फ़ोन, रक्त समूह, जन्म तिथि आदि)

## English

**Data about app users:** The app does not collect personal data from you. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or
tracking SDKs. Your preferences (e.g. reminder time) and downloaded lists are stored only on your device and are
deleted when you uninstall the app.

**Network use:** The app periodically downloads information published by the organisation (member directory, emergency
contacts, notices/advertisements, festival lists) from Google Firebase. No identifier is sent. As with any web request,
Google's servers can see your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

**Maheshwari Directory:** The directory displays community members' details (name, parents' names, city, phone, blood
group, date of birth, etc.) publicly. The purpose is to help members contact each other and find blood donors in
emergencies. Authorised administrators of the organisation publish it with members' permission. To correct or remove
your entry, contact [CONTACT]. Changes reach devices when they next connect to the internet.

**Administrators:** The separate admin portal uses Firebase Authentication (email/password) for authorised
administrators only. Their actions are logged.

**Notification permission:** Used only for local festival reminders, scheduled on your device.

**Calls and links:** Call and link buttons open your dialer, WhatsApp, email or browser only when you tap them. Those
services' own policies apply.

The app is not directed at children under 13.
